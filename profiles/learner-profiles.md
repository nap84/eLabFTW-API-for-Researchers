## Target Audience

The target audience includes researchers at any level who are already familiar with working with eLabFTW who want to gain the technical skills required to automate data workflows between their local work and the ELN, with the intention of fully documenting the research process. They want a basic understanding of the options available, using examples and tutorials that are flexible enough for them to modify to their own situation.

### Notes

Participants must already be eLabFTW users, since they should know how the notebook is structured and the terminology is uses.

While it may be helpful to have some programming skills, I want to empower programming novices to learn some basics and solve their workflow problems.
